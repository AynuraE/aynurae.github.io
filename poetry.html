Untitled

in Uzbek

Bulutlarni quchmoqlikni xohlaydi bu ko’ngil, Qushlardek parvoz qilish tugundek qadalgan-ku! Nahot uchmoqlikka kerakdir qanot? Xayollarda parvoz qilish imkoniyati ham berilgan-ku!

Aral Sea

Dear Aral Sea,I cannot believe it’s true… I cannot believe in my eyes; as if all of this is a nightmare…Where’s your azure sea? All I see is disaster and black, No matter how hard we try, You seem not to want to come back…You were inspiration and a source of life, With nutritious fish and animal kingdom, What do you actually want from us? Sometimes peace, and sometimes strife…We continue believing that you’ll come back, You’re so strong to bounce it back, I am very uncertain, but I just hope… And it is totally free to dare to hope!Please come back, My dear, Aral!

Stranger

Mom, I’m leaving home to a country in another continent, where very few know my name, where I’m a foreigner.I think I’ll make some friends and maybe lose some from home. I’m going to a first world, where the money you and dad earned, valued for only two weeks of groceries.But don’t worry! I will be fine. I don’t know what awaits me, What kind of struggles will I overcome? But I know you’ll be proud of me, For all the reasons I’ve made so far.Mom, we’ll be ten hours apart, You’ll go to sleep earlier than me. Now it’s the time for us to part, When we meet again, it’ll already be one year.

Love

Recently, while in my room, I realized my room is a reflection of my past. The past self of me, who grew up in order In fact, my whole life is itself orderly. Put back the same cup at the exact table; Socks, too, should quickly find their place.Why don’t I tell myself that I can go to bed a little early or late, and that my routine all the time doesn’t have to be the same?I’m afraid. You see, I grew up following orders, and that applies to how I see love. Because love, as far as I’ve been told, is all chaos. It’s a storm that stretches your soul and leaves you, laughing byDo I want love in my life? Do I want chaos in my life? And I know, there’s beauty as well in love; To be capable of love and deserving to be loved They’re equally powerful, I think.As I told you: Being loved not as a daughter, not as a friend, but as someone’s personal choice is such a wonderful thing.Love is chaos not just in my life but in my mind as well. How can I stop thinking… About what you like to have for breakfast What you think about when you go to bed And I want to know at that time if I ever cross your mind?How can I stop thinking… About what your dreams are And whether you share them with someone at all My only wish is to see you achieve them all And do you allow me to contribute?Being loved as someone’s personal choice is such a wonderful thing.

For brother

I have an early memory of you: us playing together you, sister, and IThe more we grew the more you distanced yourself from us from papa and mamaI barely knew what you were doing outside of home and school how you spent your time who your friends were Were they really your friends?You started a few habits like failing school and blaming papa and mama for being poor I hated you.You smoked occasionally hiding it from papa and mama you thought we were fools we smelled the cigarettes every time you walked in and you pretended we didn’tI can’t fathom to this day why you never liked me much why love came easier for our other siblingI can’t fathom to this day why you were never proud of what I did at school or at home what I liked was “for boys” what I reached for wasn’t for me I’m not proud of you in return.I’m a continent away from you yet you never checked in the same way you never seemed to care I am learning how to become careless tooI can’t fathom to this day how two siblings from the same blood can become worlds apartWhen I returned home after a year you never cared about what I saw who I became friends with what made me get up in the morning what made me smileDon’t care about me Don’t check in Don’t say a single word Just let me be.
