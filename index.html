About

Sa’lem!

My name is Aynura. My name translates to Moonlight (Ay – moon, nura – light) from Karakalpak.

I’m from Karakalpakstan, an autonomous republic within Uzbekistan, Central Asia. I am currently pursuing a Bachelor’s Degree at Dartmouth College. I’m majoring in Cognitive Science with a focus on Cognitive Science of Design and minoring in Anthropology.

I believe each person is an open book and we should take time to read them carefully. This website offers a personal glimpse into who I am—beyond what bullet points on a resume can offer.

Raxmet!

E.E. Just Program & Digital Applied Learning and Innovation (DALI) Lab

Conducted 25+ hours of semi-structured interviews with Columbia University PhD researchers and teachers in New Zealand to understand Māori language preservation efforts. Synthesized the findings into design requirements for an interactive language-learning website, then built it in React and HTML, presenting Māori content in five formats: ASR, TTS, parser, forced alignment, and spellcheck.
